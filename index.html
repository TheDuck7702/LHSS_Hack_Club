Why Join LHSS Hack Club?

Not another boring lecture club. You build real projects and get rewarded with real tech grants.

Learn by Building

No prerequisites or prior coding experience needed! We learn together by shipping real web apps, Discord bots, and games.

YSWS Hardware Perks

Through Hack Club's "You Ship, We Shell" (YSWS) program, submit your finished projects to get free custom domain names, PCB manufacturing, 3D printing grants, or Raspberry Pis!

Waterloo Tech Community

Connect with fellow Laurel Heights students, join worldwide Hack Club Slack channels, collaborate on hackathons, and build your technical portfolio.

You Ship, We Shell

Get Real Tech Funded by Hack Club Global

Whenever you build a project during our LHSS Hack Club meetings and publish it online, Hack Club sends you free hardware, domains, and developer tools directly to your doorstep.

FuseRing

Onboard CAD & 3D Print
